🌟 About Ludo Don

Ludo Don is an open-source Progressive Web App (PWA) Ludo game designed to deliver an engaging, intelligent, and feature-rich gaming experience.

It goes beyond traditional Ludo gameplay by combining intelligent AI opponents, team-based partner gameplay, automatic player replacement, interactive dice animations, flexible move selection, and reliable game recovery.

Whether you want to play alone against AI, enjoy an offline match with friends, or team up with a partner, Ludo Don brings multiple ways to enjoy the classic Ludo experience.

The goal is simple: make Ludo smarter, more enjoyable, and more convenient for everyone.

✨ Key Features

🎮 1. Play with Computer

Challenge intelligent AI opponents with different difficulty-independent player configurations:

- You vs 1 Computer
- You vs 2 Computers
- You vs 3 Computers
- Custom Game

The AI does not simply make random moves. It evaluates possible moves and uses strategic decision-making.

🧠 2. Intelligent AI System

Ludo Don features a shared AI decision-making engine that adapts its behavior according to the current game mode and team relationships.

AI capabilities include:

- Threat detection and risk assessment.
- Identifying opportunities to capture opponent tokens.
- Finding and occupying safe positions.
- Evaluating possible moves before making decisions.
- Waiting for strategic opportunities to capture opponents.
- Making decisions based on the current board situation.

The AI uses the same underlying intelligence while adapting its decisions to the rules of each game mode.

🤝 3. Play as Partners

Team up and compete in partner-based matches.

Available configurations:

- You + Computer vs 2 Computers
- 2 Friends vs 2 Computers
- 2 Friends vs 2 Friends

Smart Partner AI:

When the computer is your teammate, it understands team relationships.

- It recognizes teammate tokens.
- It avoids capturing its own teammate.
- It follows partner-specific protection rules.
- It makes strategic decisions to support the team.

The objective is to work together rather than compete against your own partner.

👥 4. Play Offline

Enjoy local multiplayer without requiring an internet connection.

Available options:

- 2 Players
- 3 Players
- 4 Players
- Custom Game

Play with friends and family on the same device.

🔄 5. AI Player Takeover

Players sometimes need to leave a game temporarily.

Ludo Don provides a convenient solution.

Let Play with AI

- Open the gear/settings menu beside the dice.
- Select "Let Play with AI".
- The AI takes control of the player's turn.
- When the player returns, select "I'm Back".
- Control is handed back to the human player.

The game continues without requiring other players to wait.

🤝 6. Smart Partner Replacement

Partner mode includes a special player replacement feature.

If a human player decides to leave a partner match:

- Select "Leave Game".
- The player's tokens remain on the board.
- The AI takes control of that player's position.
- The partner match continues normally.
- The remaining teammate does not lose their partner simply because someone leaves.

This feature helps preserve the balance and continuity of team gameplay.

🎲 7. Interactive Dice Animation

Ludo Don features an animated dice experience.

- The dice rises when tapped.
- It rotates during the animation.
- It falls back in the same direction.
- The final dice result determines the displayed number.
- MP3 sound effects enhance the experience.

The animation is designed to make rolling the dice feel more engaging and realistic.

🎯 8. Flexible Token Movement

Ludo Don supports flexible move selection.

When multiple valid dice values are available:

- Tap the token you want to move.
- A small selection popup appears above the selected token.
- All available dice values are displayed.
- Choose the desired number.
- The selected token moves according to that choice.

This gives players more control over their strategic decisions.

🎲 9. Automatic Token Opening

The game supports automatic token opening according to its six-based opening rules.

When a player has no token outside the home area and rolls a six, the game can automatically open a token.

The game also supports the configured consecutive-six rules.

💾 10. Resume Last Game — Automatic Game Recovery

One of Ludo Don's most useful features is its game recovery system.

Your latest game is preserved across supported game modes.

If you accidentally close the application or remove it from the recent apps screen:

1. Open Ludo Don again.
2. Select Resume Last Game.
3. Continue playing from where you left off.

The saved game state includes important gameplay information such as:

- Token positions.
- Current player's turn.
- Dice and move state.
- Game mode.
- Player and partner configuration.
- Other relevant gameplay state.

Whether you were playing against computers, offline with friends, or in partner mode, your latest game is designed to remain recoverable.

📱 11. Progressive Web App (PWA)

Ludo Don is designed as a Progressive Web App.

- Mobile-friendly interface.
- Full-screen standalone experience.
- Installable application experience.
- Portrait-oriented game interface.
- Immersive gameplay without the usual browser interface when supported.
